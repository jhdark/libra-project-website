BABY, the bench-scale stage

Molten salt irradiated by 14 MeV neutrons from a DT generator. Bred tritium is carried out by a sweep gas and counted by liquid scintillation.

Read the BABY guide
Breeder volume
100 mL and 1 L
Breeders tested
Molten salt, FLiBe, LiPb, Li oxide (TBC)
Neutron source
DT neutron generator (14 MeV)
Tritium measurement
Liquid scintillation counting
Neutron diagnostics
Activation foils, proton recoil, diamond detector
