## Overview

The LIBRA-PI Nozzle Testing (PINT) testbed will serve as a platform to study gas sparging in molten salts for tritium extraction. Utilizing COTS components for modularitly and rapid iteration, different sparger designs will be used in various operating conditions (temperature ranges, gas pressures, etc.) to study how sparging affects (relative) tritium release over time. Additionally, this testbed will allow us to address engineering challenges in molten salt sparging and visually inspect dynamics using vacuum viewports. Ultimately, this testbed will inform and complement LIBRA-Pi's sparging method for tritium extraction in large (100L) salt volumes.
