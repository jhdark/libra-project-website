On <mark>22 July 2026</mark> the team ran a tritium breeding experiment with hot
FLiBe irradiated by DT fusion neutrons, the first irradiation of molten FLiBe
in BABY 1 L. Data and analysis are being collected in
[FLiBe-1L-run-3](https://github.com/LIBRA-project/FLiBe-1L-run-3).

<mark>The date is from the run's data files and neutron generator logs; the
repository description says 21 June 2026. TBC by the team.</mark>
